TOPICS
Week 1: Logic, quantifiers, proof technique
Week 2: Limits, the epsilon-delta definition
Week 3: Continuity, intermediate value theorem
Week 4: Derivatives from first principles
Week 5: Mean value theorem and its consequences
Week 6: Curve sketching, optimisation
Week 7: Riemann integration
Week 8: Fundamental theorem of calculus
Week 9: Techniques of integration
Week 10: Sequences and series
Week 11: Power series, Taylor's theorem
Week 12: Review

This course assumes no prior exposure to proofs. Expect the workload to be
front-loaded; students who fall behind in the first six weeks rarely recover.
