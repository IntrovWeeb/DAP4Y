WEEKLY OUTLINE
Week 1: Asymptotic analysis, recurrences, Master theorem
Week 2: Divide and conquer, mergesort, closest pair
Week 3: Greedy algorithms, exchange arguments, matroids
Week 4: Dynamic programming, memoisation, optimal substructure
Week 5: Graph traversal - BFS, DFS, topological sort
Week 6: Shortest paths - Dijkstra, Bellman-Ford
Week 7: Minimum spanning trees - Kruskal, Prim, union-find
Week 8: Network flow, max-flow min-cut, Ford-Fulkerson
Week 9: NP-completeness, reductions, SAT
Week 10: Approximation algorithms
Week 11: Randomised algorithms, concentration bounds
Week 12: Review

Late policy: 10% per day, max 3 days. No collaboration on exams.
